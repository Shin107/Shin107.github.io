About

01

Ph.D. Candidate in Physics
Department of Astronomy & Astrophysics, TIFR

I am a computational cosmologist at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, working on model-independent tests of the standard cosmological model using Type Ia supernovae and large-scale structure surveys.

My thesis work concerns the anisotropy of the local Universe: I have shown that the Pantheon+ supernova compilation carries a significant dipolar signal in both the expansion rate and the deceleration parameter, and that correcting these supernovae for progenitor-age evolution changes the inferred expansion history.

I am a member of the LSST Dark Energy Science Collaboration (DESC), and I also build mock catalogues for the Fundamental Plane sample of the DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys.

Anisotropy and progenitor-age bias in the Pantheon+ supernova compilation

2023 – 2026

with Dr. Mohamed Rameez (TIFR), Prof. Subir Sarkar (University of Oxford), and Prof. Christos Tsagas (Aristotle University of Thessaloniki)

  • Detected a dipolar anisotropy in the Hubble expansion rate of the Pantheon+ Type Ia supernova catalogue exceeding 1.5 km/s/Mpc — larger than the claimed 1% uncertainty of the SH0ES measurement — with the deceleration parameter showing dipolar modulation at 75σ significance, using Maximum Likelihood Estimation across the heliocentric, CMB, and Local Group frames.
  • Showed that correcting for Type Ia supernova progenitor-age evolution shifts the monopole deceleration parameter to positive values, indicating that the isotropic expansion of the Universe is decelerating rather than accelerating.

Tests of tilted cosmology

2025 – Present

with Dr. Mohamed Rameez (TIFR)

  • Leading development of a model-independent pipeline to test tilted ΛCDM cosmologies against upcoming LSST supernova data, validated using realistic simulations from SNANA.

Mock catalogues for the DESI Fundamental Plane sample

2025 – Present

with Dr. Shadab Alam (TIFR)

  • Building realistic mock catalogues for a photometric sample of ~480,000 early-type galaxies from the DESI Legacy Imaging Survey, using the AbacusSummit N-body simulation suite as the underlying dark-matter field.
